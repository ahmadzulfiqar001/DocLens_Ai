INDEPENDENT CONSULTING AGREEMENT

[Para 1]
This Consulting Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of September 15, 2026, by and between Apex Digital Innovations LLC ("Client"), and Vanguard Systems Inc. ("Consultant").

[Para 2]
1. Scope of Services
Consultant shall provide enterprise software architectural review and optimization consulting as mutually agreed upon in ongoing project communication. Consultant agrees to perform the services in good faith and with reasonable diligence.

[Para 3]
2. Compensation and Payment Terms
Payment will be made promptly upon completion of work without delay. The total project fee is estimated at $18,500 USD, payable upon submission of invoice.

[Para 4]
3. Term and Duration
This Agreement shall be effective starting October 1, 2026, and shall continue for a period of twelve (12) months from the effective date.

[Para 5]
4. Consultant Responsibilities & Obligations
Consultant will deliver architectural diagrams, review source code repositories, and participate in weekly sprint reviews with Client engineering leads.

[Para 6]
5. Governing Law
This Agreement and any dispute arising from it shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of the State of California, United States of America.
